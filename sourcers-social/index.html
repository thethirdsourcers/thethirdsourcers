One dashboard for your social media

Sourcers Social helps businesses plan and publish content to their social accounts, and answer customer messages with an AI assistant that hands over to a person when it should.

Request access How we handle your data

Publish everywhere

Write a post once, add photos or video, and publish it now or on a schedule to your connected Facebook Page, Instagram account, Threads profile and TikTok account.

One inbox

Messages from Facebook Messenger, Instagram and your website chat arrive in one shared inbox for your team.

AI replies from your own knowledge

An AI assistant answers common questions using the product and policy information you add. When it is unsure, the customer is upset, or they ask for a person, the conversation is handed to your team.

You stay in control

You choose which accounts to connect and can disconnect them at any time. Disconnecting revokes our access and deletes the data we stored from that account.

Delete your data

Last updated 29 September 2026

You can remove your data from Sourcers Social at any time. Pick whichever option fits.

Option 1 — Disconnect one account

  1. Sign in to Sourcers Social.
  2. Go to Channels.
  3. Select the Facebook Page, Instagram, Threads or TikTok account and choose Disconnect.

We revoke our access token and delete the messages, content and profile data we stored from that account.

Option 2 — Delete your account or workspace

  1. Sign in and open Settings → Your account.
  2. Choose Delete account. If you own a workspace, you can hand it over to another member or delete the whole workspace.

Deleting a workspace removes all its connected accounts, conversations, content and knowledge base.

Option 3 — Remove the app from Facebook, Instagram or TikTok

  • Facebook: Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations (or Apps and websites) → select Sourcers Social → Remove.
  • Instagram: Settings → Website permissions / Apps and websites → select Sourcers Social → Remove.
  • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services permissions → select Sourcers Social → Remove access.

When a platform tells us you removed the app, we delete the data we received from that account. If you want to be sure everything is gone, also send us a request (Option 4).

Option 4 — Ask us

Email hello@thethirdsourcers.com with the subject "Sourcers Social data deletion request" and include the email you sign in with, or the name of the Page or account you connected. We confirm the deletion by email.

How long it takes: data is removed from our active systems within 30 days of your request. We may keep limited records where the law requires it (for example billing records), and nothing else.

Customers who messaged a business: if you messaged a business that uses Sourcers Social, contact that business, or email us and we will help.

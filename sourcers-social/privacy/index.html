Privacy Policy

Effective 29 September 2026 · Last updated 29 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Third Sourcers Private Limited ("Third Sourcers", "we", "us") collects, uses, shares and protects information when you use Sourcers Social (the "Service") — our web application for publishing content to social media and managing customer messages with an AI assistant.

Data controller: Third Sourcers Private Limited (थर्ड सोर्सर्स प्राइभेट लिमिटेड), Company Reg. No. 401634/83/84, PAN 624715513, Ward No. 8, Bholdhoka, Lalitpur Metropolitan City, Lalitpur, Bagmati Province, Nepal.
Contact: hello@thethirdsourcers.com · +977 9808546858

1. Who this policy covers

  • Customers and their team members — businesses that create a Sourcers Social workspace, and the people they invite.
  • End customers — people who send messages to a business that uses Sourcers Social (for example through Facebook Messenger, Instagram or a website chat). For these messages the business is responsible for its own customer relationship; we process the messages on the business's behalf to provide the Service.

2. Information we collect

Account information

Your name, email address, profile picture and sign-in identifier when you create an account or sign in (including through Google via Firebase Authentication), your workspace name, the role you hold, and your settings.

Information from connected social accounts

When you choose to connect a social account, you authorize us through that platform's official login and permission screen. We only receive the data covered by the permissions you approve:

PlatformData we receive and use
FacebookYour name and user ID; the list of Pages you choose to connect, their names, IDs and Page access tokens; messages sent to those Pages and replies sent from them; posts you publish through the Service.
InstagramThe professional account's ID, username and profile picture; direct messages sent to the account and replies sent from it; content you publish through the Service.
ThreadsYour Threads user ID, username and profile picture; posts you publish through the Service.
TikTokBasic profile information (open ID, display name and avatar) through Login Kit; the videos you choose to upload or publish, their captions and privacy settings, and the resulting post status through the Content Posting API.

We do not receive your social media passwords. Access tokens are stored so the Service can act only on the accounts you connected and only for the purposes described here.

Messages and content

The text, images, voice notes and attachments in conversations that reach your connected accounts, the sender's platform ID, display name and profile picture where the platform provides them, and the content you create, schedule or publish in the Service.

Knowledge base

Text, documents, images and website pages you add so the AI assistant can answer questions about your business.

Usage and technical information

Log data such as IP address, browser type, pages used, timestamps and errors, and — if you turn them on — browser push-notification subscriptions for your devices.

3. How we use information

  • To provide the Service: connect your accounts, publish and schedule your content, show incoming messages in your inbox and send your replies.
  • To generate AI-suggested or automatic replies from your knowledge base, detect when a conversation should be handed to a person, and transcribe voice messages.
  • To notify you and your team about new or escalated conversations.
  • To keep the Service secure, prevent abuse, and fix problems.
  • To communicate with you about your account, including service and security emails.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We use data obtained from Meta (Facebook, Instagram, Threads) and TikTok only to provide the features you use in the Service. We do not sell this data, use it for advertising, build profiles of end customers, or use it to train general-purpose AI models.

4. How we share information

We share information only as needed to run the Service:

  • The social platforms you connect (Meta and TikTok) — to publish your content and send your replies.
  • Service providers that process data on our behalf under confidentiality obligations: cloud hosting and database providers, Cloudflare (network and security), Google Firebase (sign-in), Resend (email delivery), browser push services (notifications), and AI model providers such as OpenRouter that generate replies or text embeddings. Some AI processing may run on models we host ourselves.
  • Your own workspace — members of a workspace can see the conversations, content and connected accounts of that workspace according to their role.
  • Legal reasons — when required by law, or to protect the rights, safety and security of users, the public or Third Sourcers.
  • Business transfers — if Third Sourcers is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, subject to this policy.

5. Retention

  • Connected-account tokens are kept until you disconnect the account or delete your workspace.
  • Messages, content and knowledge-base data are kept while your workspace is active so you can use your history, and deleted when you delete them, disconnect the related account, or delete your workspace.
  • After deletion, data is removed from our active systems within 30 days; limited information may be kept longer where the law requires it (for example billing records).

6. Deleting your data

You can disconnect any social account inside Sourcers Social, delete your account or workspace from Settings, or ask us to delete your data. Full instructions, including for people who only removed the app from Facebook, Instagram or TikTok, are on our Data Deletion page. You can also revoke our access at any time from your Facebook, Instagram, Threads or TikTok settings.

7. Security

We use HTTPS for all connections, access controls based on workspace roles, signature verification of platform webhooks, encryption for push notifications, and restricted access to production systems. No system is perfectly secure, and we will notify affected users and authorities of a data breach as required by law.

8. Your rights

Subject to applicable law — including the Privacy Act, 2075 (2018) of Nepal and, where it applies to you, the EU/UK GDPR — you may ask to access, correct, export or delete your personal information, object to or restrict certain processing, and withdraw consent. Email hello@thethirdsourcers.com; we respond within 30 days. If you are an end customer of a business that uses Sourcers Social, you can also contact that business directly.

9. International transfers

We are based in Nepal and our service providers may process data in other countries. Where we transfer personal information across borders we use providers that offer appropriate safeguards.

10. Children

The Service is for businesses and is not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the Service.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will change the "Last updated" date above and, for material changes, notify workspace owners by email or in the Service before the change takes effect.
